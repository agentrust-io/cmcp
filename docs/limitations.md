This page lists what cMCP does not protect against, where its promises stop, and what you have to handle with other controls. Read it before you rely on a cMCP receipt for a security or compliance decision. Each limit names the part of the system it applies to; terms such as TEE (sealed-off hardware) and attestation (a hardware-signed report of what is running) are explained in [the terms, in plain English](https://agentrust-io.com/#plain-terms).
